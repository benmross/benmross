### Now

- **[Herald](https://github.com/benmross/herald)**: an open-source personal agent that runs on your own machine. It reads mail, calendar, messages, location and health into one ledger, and anything another person would see waits for a tap on your phone.
- **[loop-s2](https://github.com/benmross/loop-s2)**: operator console for the URC Autonomous Navigation Mission, ROS 2 Jazzy, built for UMD Loop Challenge Week.
- **Planet Falconia**, a VR digital twin of a rover mission built with NIST, accepted at the SIGGRAPH Asia 2026 Educator's Forum.

### Built

- **SMCS Kart** ([gokart-dash](https://github.com/PHS-SMCS/gokart-dash)): a drive-by-wire electric go-kart, pitched to replace the robotics curriculum at Poolesville High School and taught from as it was built. Teensy 4.1, ESP32 steer-by-wire over CAN, a React dashboard on a Raspberry Pi. 40 mph, 40 hp peak.
- **GNSS spoofing detection**, Army Research Laboratory: finding where GPS is being spoofed from the position reports ships and aircraft already broadcast.
- **Sparrows**: schedules, clubs and announcements for 30+ schools in Montgomery County, about 30,000 monthly users. Lead developer, 2023 to 2026.
- **BenGPT**: Llama 3.1 fine-tuned on five years of my own text messages. My best friend could not reliably tell it from me.
- **[emulsion](https://github.com/benmross/emulsion)**: film grain generated from math in one WebGL2 shader. [Try it](https://emulsion.benmross.com).
- **[PartyPad](https://github.com/benmross/partypad)**: phone browsers as motion controllers for Dolphin.
- Earlier: an autonomous blimp, the lead programming on a rover for a simulated exoplanet, and a Next.js and Stripe platform for a nonprofit.
