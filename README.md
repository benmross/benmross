### Now

- **[Herald](https://github.com/benmross/herald)**: an open-source personal agent that runs on your own machine. It reads mail, calendar, messages, location and health into one ledger, and anything another person would see waits for a tap on your phone.
- **UMD Loop** (University Rover Challenge): the Phase II operator console and mission manager for the team's ROS 2 navigation stack ([loop-phase2](https://github.com/benmross/loop-phase2)), and two ways to drive the rover arm with no controller: ARKit on a handheld iPhone, and 3-D hand tracking from two ordinary cameras.
- **App Dev Club**, Technical Operations: Stripe virtual cards so each subteam can spend from the central budget.
- **iOS apps** in SwiftUI and Metal: a dining app that sorts UMD menus against food allergies, and a camera that merges bursts of RAW frames to look like a Sony a6400.

### Built

- **SMCS Kart** ([gokart-dash](https://github.com/PHS-SMCS/gokart-dash)): a drive-by-wire electric go-kart, pitched to replace the robotics curriculum at Poolesville High School and taught from as it was built, to a class of 30. All of the firmware: a Teensy 4.1 that owns every motion decision, ESP32 steer-by-wire over CAN, and a React dashboard on a Raspberry Pi that can crash without affecting driving. 40 mph.
- **GNSS spoofing detection**, Army Research Laboratory: finding where GPS is jammed or spoofed from the position reports ships and aircraft already broadcast, about 100 million a day.
- **Chalgen**: an agentic harness in which an LLM writes, runs and checks its own cyber forensics challenges. Montgomery College ran its March 2026 competition on it.
- **Sparrows**: schedules, clubs and announcements for 30+ schools in Montgomery County, about 30,000 monthly users. Lead developer, 2023 to 2026.
- **BenGPT**: Llama 3.1 8B fine-tuned on five years of my own text messages. My best friend could not reliably tell it from me.
- **Planet Falconia**: a VR digital twin of a rover mission, built with NIST and accepted at the SIGGRAPH Asia 2026 Educator's Forum.
- **[emulsion](https://github.com/benmross/emulsion)**: film grain generated from math in one WebGL2 shader. [Try it](https://emulsion.benmross.com).
- **[PartyPad](https://github.com/benmross/partypad)**: phone browsers as motion controllers for Dolphin, signalled through Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects.
